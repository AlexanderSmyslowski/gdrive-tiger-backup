# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Mac users, freelancers, and small teams who keep important work in Google Drive and want an independent local copy on an external disk or NAS. The primary experience must work for non-technical users, while retaining transparent logs and advanced controls for power users.

## Product Purpose

Create a trustworthy, independent Google Drive backup without modifying Drive. Success means users can see that their data was backed up, understand where and how it is protected, recover an older file without terminal knowledge, and distinguish a verified success from a failure or cancellation.

## Brand Personality

Calm, independent, and charming. The product should feel reassuring during a high-trust task, distinctly Mac-native, and lightly nostalgic without becoming a novelty.

## Anti-references

- A retro mockup that prioritizes visual imitation over native behavior, accessibility, or trust.
- A Time Machine clone that implies complete point-in-time snapshots the product does not create.
- A thin graphical wrapper that still requires users to understand shell commands, mount paths, launchd, or raw rclone errors.
- A generic enterprise backup console that loses the product's small, personal Mac character.

## Design Principles

- Tell the truth about backup state: success, failure, and cancellation must never be visually interchangeable.
- Make recovery a first-class workflow; a backup is not trustworthy until a user can restore from it.
- Use nostalgia as a signature, not as an excuse to reinvent standard Mac controls.
- Show safety and destination health in plain language before asking the user to act.
- Keep strong defaults simple, with technical detail available progressively rather than exposed by default.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

VoiceOver and complete keyboard operation are release requirements. Respect Reduce Motion, Increase Contrast, and system appearance settings; do not rely on color alone; keep text and controls readable at larger accessibility sizes where applicable. Target WCAG 2.2 AA principles for relevant visual and interaction behavior while following native macOS accessibility conventions.
